## Elevator framing (what this is)

Cosentus offers a **suite of specialized AI voice agents** for **healthcare revenue cycle management (RCM)**. Instead of one generic bot, the product is **eight role-specific agents** that cover **patient-facing** and **payer-facing** work across the cycle—from scheduling and pre-service financial conversations through eligibility, prior auth, claims, and payment / reconciliation. Voice is **real-time**; the stack is built for **integration** (e.g. web embeds and programmatic use), not only a static demo.

**Platform note (factual):** Voice is powered by **Retell AI**; positioning should emphasize **vertical RCM depth** and **workflow coverage**, not the underlying vendor.

---

## Why this matters to investors (angles, not claims)

- **Labor and scale:** Automates high-volume, repetitive phone work (scheduling, verification, follow-up, patient financial conversations) that otherwise scales with headcount.
- **Full-cycle coverage:** Same platform spans **front office**, **payer operations**, and **back-office** reconciliation-style workflows—suggesting **expandable ACV** and cross-sell within a practice or platform customer.
- **Enterprise fit:** Designed around **healthcare compliance** (e.g. **HIPAA-aligned** positioning) and **real integrations**, not a consumer toy.
- **Defensibility (story):** Depth in **RCM workflows and language** (including **multilingual** patient-facing use where applicable) vs. horizontal voice APIs or generic assistants.

## The eight agents (names + one line each)

| Agent   | One-line role |
|--------|----------------|
| **Cindy**   | Patient **payment & balance** — balances, payments, coverage explanation; strong **multilingual** and high-concurrency patient support. |
| **Chris**   | **Outbound** insurance **claim follow-up** and denial-related work with carriers. |
| **Emily**   | **Pre-service anesthesia** payment options (insured / self-pay / cosmetic), plans and facility-aware pricing. |
| **Sarah**   | **Appointment scheduling** — inbound and outbound, calendar-oriented. |
| **Allison** | **General patient / front-office** support — inquiries, routing, practice information, overflow / after-hours style coverage. |
| **Harper**  | **Outbound** **eligibility & benefits** verification with payers before service. |
| **Olivia**  | **Outbound** **prior authorization** status tracking and urgency / documentation. |
| **Michael** | **Payer-side payment reconciliation** — missing / partial / incorrect payments, EOBs, discrepancy resolution. |

**RCM span (summary):** Pre-service (Harper, Olivia, Emily, Sarah) → delivery / front desk (Sarah, Allison) → post-service and payer (Chris, Michael, Cindy, Allison).

**Call direction (summary):** Inbound-heavy: Cindy, Allison, Emily, Sarah. Outbound-heavy: Chris, Harper, Olivia, Michael. Sarah does both.
